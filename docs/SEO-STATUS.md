# Search visibility — 11 September 2026

Production: https://www.everestsuperchemical.com.np/

## Implemented

- Homepage WebSite JSON-LD names Everest Super Chemical Udhyog, with Everest Super Chemical and ESCU as alternatives. It links to the existing local business entity.
- Full company name in page title templates; individual product supplier titles and descriptions.
- Distinct category metadata, headings, descriptions and self-canonical URLs for the three existing category views. Their structured product lists now match the products displayed.
- Updated sitemap with 36 URLs and the actual content-change date.
- More explicit, visible Nepal chemical-supply copy while preserving the hero and intro animation.
- Removed unsupported manufacturer/brand claims and logo-as-product-image markup from imported/generic products. Product rich results still require genuine pricing or qualifying reviews; do not fabricate them.
- Build and focused lint passed. All 36 production sitemap URLs returned 200, had a title, canonical URL, one H1, parseable JSON-LD and no page-level noindex.

## Search Console observations

The Page indexing report (last updated 4 September) lists 20 indexed URLs, 13 discovered but not indexed, and 2 redirects. Redirect exclusions are expected. Liquid Soap's individual inspection reports that it is not indexed; Google accepted priority indexing requests for Liquid Soap and the homepage on 11 September. Sitemap resubmission succeeded.

The Performance screen with the three-month range selected displayed 35 clicks, 555 impressions, 6.3% CTR and average position 10.8. The rendered chart covers 27 July–8 September. These are aggregate figures, not the ranking for a specific chemical query. Queries include chemical suppliers in Kathmandu, bleaching powder, hydrochloric acid price in Nepal and liquid chlorine.

## Follow-through

Review indexed URLs and Nepal-filtered queries after Google processes the changes. Compare date ranges rather than personal search screenshots. Prioritize Liquid Soap and the relevant category pages, then product pages with impressions but few clicks.

Add actual product packaging photos, verified grades, pack sizes and availability as the business supplies them. Published prices, certificates, SDS downloads, reviews and manufacturer names must be real and kept current. Keep the Google Business Profile's company name, address, phone and website consistent with the site; do not add keywords to the registered business name. Earn links from real customers, suppliers and relevant business listings rather than buying ranking links.

Google controls whether and when it selects the preferred site name, indexes pages and ranks them. This implementation does not promise a position or an indexing deadline.
