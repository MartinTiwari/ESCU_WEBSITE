# ESCU: SEO, GEO and AEO with CITE

CITE here follows [Citable's published framework](https://citable.agency/framework/): Crawl access, Identity, Trust signals and Extractability. It is an audit structure, not an official Google ranking system or a guarantee of citations.

## Changes

| Pillar | Implementation | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crawl access | Added /buying-guide to the generated sitemap and site-wide navigation. Retained server-rendered content, existing production indexing rules, canonical URLs and preview exclusions. | Actual crawler access through CDN/bot protections and search indexing are separate checks. No firewall or training-bot policy changed. |
| Identity | Connected the owner-confirmed Instagram profile through visible footer navigation and business sameAs markup; normalized the structured-data phone to international format. | No invented profile, association membership, review, certificate or knowledge-graph identity. |
| Trust signals | Buying answers identify the business, contact details and quote process; availability and delivery require confirmation. | These remain first-party statements, not independent endorsements. External evidence still needs to be earned and verified. |
| Extractability | Added seven direct, server-rendered buying answers with matching FAQPage data, stable question anchors and contextual links from all product pages. Clarified the homepage's description of what ESCU supplies. | FAQPage describes the visible answers semantically; Google retired FAQ rich results in May 2026, so no such appearance is promised. No AI-specific markup can guarantee inclusion. |

## Evidence still needed from the business

- Actual shop, stock, packing and team photographs, with permission where appropriate.
- Confirm and document the About page's current claims about every-batch checking, stable bulk prices, delivery reach and staff/warehouse anecdotes before using them in additional search content.
- Real product-specific specifications and safety data sheets; do not manufacture grades, certifications or batch results.
- Genuine Google customer reviews; no incentives or review gating.
- Existing supplier/association listings that accurately identify ESCU. Outreach drafts exist in the prior SEO checklist; no messages were sent.

## Measurement

After deployment, verify /buying-guide in Search Console and observe crawl/index status. Compare relevant search impressions, clicks and qualified enquiries over comparable periods. Test a fixed set of buyer questions in the answer engines you use and record date, exact prompt, response, cited URL and whether ESCU is mentioned. There is no citation baseline measured in this change.

Suggested questions: Where is Everest Super Chemical Udhyog? How do I request a bulk chemical quote in Kathmandu? Does ESCU deliver outside Kathmandu? Where can I buy pool and cleaning chemicals in Nepal?

The original splash animation remains unchanged at the owner's explicit request. No field Core Web Vitals improvement is claimed.
